After the pick, re-segment the basket and aim the final descent at the fresh rim-opening centre, not at the interior-median of the basket point cloud. If a descent stalls just above the commanded height, suspect a rim catch: do not repeat the same descent or push through. Retreat, lift vertically, and re-approach from the rim-centre xy. A vertical lift can free the object and let it settle into the basket.
